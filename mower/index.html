Utah winter ops (Zone 7a)

From late October through mid-April, the mower lives in your garage. Pre-storage routine takes 20 minutes:

  • Charge battery to full, then power off (lithium stores best at 50–80% — top off mid-winter once).
  • Hose off the deck (i215 is IP66), let dry, replace blades if worn.
  • Bring the charging dock indoors too — Utah freeze-thaw cycles damage exposed electronics.
  • Boundary mapping and no-go zones persist in the app; first mow in spring is plug-and-go.
