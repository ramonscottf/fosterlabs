Farmington Junior Rider License

Bicycle & Scooter Operator Examination — Davis County, Utah

The License Program

Four exams, one license. Same shape as a real Utah driver license: rules test, closed-course skills test, road test, and restrictions printed right on the card. Nothing here is a suggestion — a fail is a fail, and you take it again next week.

Applicant

The four gates

Gate 1

Pre-Ride Inspection

15-point safety check. Every item passes or the exam does not start. Same as a vehicle failing inspection at the DMV.

Gate 2

Written Knowledge Test

15 questions pulled from a 28-question bank. Real Utah law. 12 of 15 to pass (80%).

Gate 3

Closed-Course Skills

8 stations in the driveway and cul-de-sac. Scored 0–2 each. 13 of 16 to pass, and no station may score 0.

Gate 4

Road Test

16-leg route on real Farmington streets. Start at 100, lose points for errors. 80 to pass. Ten auto-fails end it instantly.

Riding zones

The license does not say "you can ride." It says where. Zones open up as scores go up — that's the whole point of the card.

1
Zone 1 — Driveway & cul-de-sac
Home pavement only. No street crossings. Open to a learner permit with a helmet on.
2
Zone 2 — The Loveland Lane block
Our street, curb to curb, no crossing an intersection. Unlocked by a Class J license.
3
Zone 3 — Neighborhood loop + Farmington Ranches Park
Interior residential streets and the park path. Requires a road-test score of 90+, a buddy, and a check-in time.
4
Zone 4 — Anything that carries real traffic
Clark Ln, Glovers Ln, 1525 W, Frontage Rd, anything toward Station Park or the FrontRunner tracks. Adult present. No exceptions, no version of the license.
Non-negotiable — costs the license immediately
  • Riding without a helmet, even once, even to the mailbox.
  • Crossing into Zone 4 without an adult.
  • Earbuds or a phone in hand while rolling.
  • Going somewhere nobody was told about.

Gate 1 · Pre-Ride Inspection

Done by the rider, out loud, with the examiner watching. Touch every part as you call it. Utah lets a peace officer stop and inspect a bike they think is unsafe (41-6a-1110) — so we do it first.

0/15
Items cleared

Gate 2 · Written Knowledge Test

15 questions, drawn at random from the bank. Answer, get the explanation immediately, move on. 12 correct to pass. Miss it, and the whole test regenerates next time — no memorizing positions.

Study sheet

Every question in the bank, with the answer and the law behind it. Read it before the test, not during.

Gate 3 · Closed-Course Skills Test

Chalk and cones in the driveway and the mouth of the cul-de-sac. Whole course fits in about 100 × 40 feet. Score each station 2 (clean) / 1 (wobbled, corrected) / 0 (failed). Need 13 of 16, and a zero anywhere is an automatic retest of that station on another day.

Setup kit: sidewalk chalk, 5 cones (soda cans and shoes work), a tape measure, a stopwatch (phone), and a whistle. Takes about 10 minutes to lay out. Chalk it once and it survives a few days of sprinklers.

Gate 4 · Road Test

The examiner follows on a bike or on foot, a bike length back and slightly left, saying nothing except directions. Start at 100 points. Every error costs. Below 80 is a fail; an auto-fail ends the test where it happens and the ride home is a walk.
